All 100 papers in one gene–disease graph100 篇论文合并为一张基因–疾病图

Every supported gene–disease link across the 100 papers, aggregated into one network. Thicker lines indicate support from more papers, while larger nodes appear in supported links across more papers. Genes and diseases are merged across papers by normalized surface form (case, hyphens, and apostrophes unified) rather than database ID, so lexical variants such as "Parkinson disease" and "Parkinson's disease" remain separate nodes.将 100 篇论文中全部 supported 的基因–疾病关系聚合为一张全局网络。支持某条连边的论文越多，连边越粗；节点越大，代表其在越多篇论文的 supported 关系中出现。基因与疾病按规范化名称跨文献合并（统一大小写、连字符与撇号），暂未映射至数据库 ID，因此 "Parkinson disease" 与 "Parkinson's disease" 等同义写法仍保留为独立节点。
