Gene–disease extraction: our method vs PubTator3

Same 12 benchmark papers. Our method reads the parsed full text with the decision card (variant v2_full_lean_medium), using one initial request and at most one repair. PubTator3 results are precomputed annotations fetched by PMID and aligned locally; its relations are primarily abstract-derived. Neither system is used as the answer key. Output counts and disagreements do not establish relative accuracy or recall.
